The Government of India notified on November 21, 2025, the four Labour Codes - the Code on Wages, 2019, the Industrial Relations Code, 2020, the Code on Social Security, 2020, and the Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020 - consolidating existing labour laws. The Group has assessed the incremental impact of these changes on the basis consistent with the Labour Codes, draft rules, FAQs and legal opinion. Considering the regulatory driven non-recurring nature, the impact has been disclosed under Exceptional Items in the consolidated financial results for the year ended March 31, 2026. The Government of India is in the process of notifying related rules to the New Labour Codes and the impact of these will be evaluated and appropriately accounted as and when notified.
